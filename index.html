Warhammer 40,000

Warhammer 40k TTRPG

Warhammer 40,000 is a grimdark science-fiction setting created by Games Workshop. It takes place in the 41st millennium, a time when humanity is spread across a million worlds and surrounded by enemies on every side. In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war.

The Warhammer 40,000 tabletop roleplaying game (TTRPG) lets players step into that universe as individual characters instead of commanding whole armies. You might play an Inquisitor hunting heretics, a soldier of the Astra Militarum, a tech-priest of Mars, a Rogue Trader exploring beyond the Imperium's borders, or something far stranger. A Game Master guides the story while players roll dice to fight, investigate, negotiate and survive.

World Lore

For over ten thousand years the God-Emperor of Mankind has sat upon the Golden Throne on Terra, kept alive by ancient machinery after his traitor son Horus nearly slew him during the Horus Heresy. His Imperium is vast, faithful and brutal, defended by the genetically engineered Space Marines and countless billions of ordinary soldiers.

Against the Imperium stand the alien Xenos, from the awakening Necron dynasties and the endless Ork WAAAGH! to the expanding T'au Empire and the all-devouring Tyranid Hive Fleets. From the Warp, the four Chaos Gods, Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle and Slaanesh, seek to corrupt mortal souls and tear reality apart.
