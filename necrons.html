Necrons

About Necrons

An ancient race of skeletal androids who once ruled the galaxy as the Necrontyr. After trading their flesh for undying metal bodies, they slept for sixty million years in stasis-tombs. Now their dynasties are awakening to reclaim the stars.

Lore

  • The Necrontyr were short-lived beings who allied with the star-gods known as the C'tan, who transferred their minds into living metal through biotransference.
  • During the Great Sundering the Necrons turned on the C'tan, shattering them into countless shards that are now bound as weapons.
  • Most rank-and-file Necrons lost their minds in the process; only the nobility retained their full personalities.
  • Many tomb worlds awoke damaged, leaving some Necron lords insane, such as those afflicted by the Flayer Virus.

Playstyle & Goals

Slow, relentless and extremely durable. Necron forces advance steadily, shrug off damage, and repair fallen warriors through Reanimation Protocols. In a roleplay setting they suit cold, calculating, aristocratic characters bound by ancient codes of honor.

Goals

  • Reclaim their lost empire and the worlds they once ruled
  • Restore the dynasties and their former glory
  • Some nobles seek a way to return to flesh bodies
  • Eliminate the threat of the Tyranids and other upstart races

Types of Troops

  • Necron Warriors: Core infantry armed with gauss flayers that strip targets apart atom by atom.
  • Immortals: Elite line troops who kept more of their minds and wield gauss blasters or tesla carbines.
  • Lychguard: Bodyguards of the nobility, armed with warscythes or hyperphase swords.
  • Crypteks: Techno-sorcerers who command the forces of physics and time.
  • Overlords and Lords: Noble commanders who lead the dynasties.
  • Canoptek Constructs: Scarabs, Wraiths and Spyders that maintain the tomb worlds.
  • Flayed Ones: Insane Necrons cursed with a hunger for flesh.
  • C'tan Shards: Enslaved fragments of star-gods unleashed as living weapons.
