Tyranids

About Tyranids

An extragalactic swarm of bio-engineered horrors that devours all biological matter in its path. Directed by a single gestalt consciousness called the Hive Mind, Tyranid Hive Fleets strip entire worlds to bare rock.

Lore

  • Tyranids arrived from beyond the galaxy in vast Hive Fleets such as Behemoth, Kraken and Leviathan.
  • Every Tyranid organism is a tool of the Hive Mind, adapted to a specific purpose.
  • Genestealers infiltrate worlds ahead of the fleets, creating Genestealer Cults that weaken planets from within.
  • The Hive Mind casts a 'Shadow in the Warp' that blocks psychic communication and terrifies psykers.
  • After consuming a world, the Tyranids digest all its biomass in pools, leaving it lifeless.

Playstyle & Goals

Overwhelming swarms supported by giant monstrous creatures. Tyranids constantly adapt to their enemies and rely on synapse creatures to keep the lesser beasts in line. In a roleplay setting they are best used as a terrifying antagonist, or for horror-themed campaigns built around survival.

Goals

  • Consume all biomass in the galaxy
  • Adapt and evolve by absorbing the genetic traits of prey
  • Expand the Hive Fleets to new worlds
  • Eliminate psychic resistance and threats to the swarm

Types of Troops

  • Termagants and Hormagaunts: Swarms of small, expendable creatures that fight at range or in melee.
  • Rippers: Tiny worm-like creatures that consume everything in their path.
  • Tyranid Warriors: Mid-sized synapse creatures that lead the swarm.
  • Genestealers: Fast, deadly infiltrators with razor-sharp claws.
  • Lictors: Camouflaged stalker-assassins that hunt prey ahead of the swarm.
  • Zoanthropes: Floating psychic creatures that blast enemies with Warp energy.
  • Carnifexes: Living battering rams built to smash armor and fortifications.
  • Hive Tyrants: Massive synaptic leaders that channel the will of the Hive Mind.
  • Trygons and Mawlocs: Burrowing monsters that ambush from below the ground.
  • Hierophants and Bio-Titans: Colossal war-beasts used in the largest invasions.
