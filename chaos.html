Chaos

About the Chaos

The Chaos Gods are entities of the Warp born from the emotions of mortals. Their followers include traitor Space Marines, cultists, mutants and daemons, all seeking to corrupt or destroy the Imperium. The four great powers are Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle and Slaanesh.

Chaos Factions

Khorne

The Blood God and Lord of Skulls, Khorne is the Chaos God of rage, war, bloodshed and martial honor. He cares not from where the blood flows, only that it flows.

Read more about Khorne

Tzeentch

The Changer of Ways and Architect of Fate, Tzeentch is the Chaos God of change, hope, ambition, sorcery and scheming. His plots are so complex that even failure may be part of his plan.

Read more about Tzeentch

Nurgle

Grandfather Nurgle is the Chaos God of disease, decay, despair and rebirth. Surprisingly jovial, he loves his followers and gifts them with plagues that make them immune to pain and fear of death.

Read more about Nurgle

Slaanesh

The Dark Prince of Excess, Slaanesh is the Chaos God of pleasure, perfection, pride and indulgence. Born from the decadence of the ancient Aeldari, Slaanesh drives followers to push every sensation to its extreme.

Read more about Slaanesh
