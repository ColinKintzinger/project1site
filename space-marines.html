Space Marines

Adeptus Astartes

About Adeptus Astartes

Each Space Marine is implanted with gene-seed derived from one of the Primarchs, the Emperor's genetically engineered sons. They wear power armor, wield bolters and fight without fear.

Lore

  • The original twenty Space Marine Legions were each led by a Primarch.
  • During the Horus Heresy, half the Legions turned to Chaos and betrayed the Emperor.
  • After the Heresy the loyal Legions were broken into smaller Chapters by the Codex Astartes, written by Roboute Guilliman.
  • Famous Chapters include the Ultramarines, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Space Wolves and Imperial Fists.
  • The Primaris Marines, created by Belisarius Cawl, are a newer and stronger generation of Space Marine.
  • Space Marines receive 19 implanted organs, including a second heart and the Black Carapace that links them to their armor.

Playstyle & Goals

Elite, well-rounded and adaptable. Space Marines are few in number but extremely durable and skilled, capable of fighting at range or in melee. In a roleplay setting they suit heroic, disciplined and devoted characters who face overwhelming odds with honor.

Goals

  • Defend humanity and the Imperium from all threats
  • Serve the Emperor and uphold the honor of their Chapter
  • Destroy xenos, heretics and Chaos
  • Preserve their gene-seed and Chapter traditions

Types of Troops

  • Intercessors: Primaris line troops armed with bolt rifles.
  • Tactical Marines: Versatile classic Space Marine infantry.
  • Assault Marines: Jump-pack troops who strike in melee.
  • Devastator Marines: Heavy weapons specialists.
  • Scouts: Lightly armored recruits who infiltrate and snipe.
  • Terminators: Veterans in heavy Tactical Dreadnought armor.
  • Librarians: Psykers who wield the Warp for the Chapter.
  • Chaplains: Spiritual leaders who keep the faith and fervor of the Chapter.
  • Apothecaries: Medics who recover the gene-seed of fallen Marines.
  • Techmarines: Engineers trained by the Adeptus Mechanicus.
  • Dreadnoughts: War machines housing mortally wounded heroes.
  • Captains and Chapter Masters: Commanders of companies and Chapters.
