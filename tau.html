T'au Empire

About T'au Empire

A young, technologically advanced and expansionist empire that believes in the 'Greater Good'. The T'au unite their own castes and many conquered or allied alien races under a single philosophy of cooperation and shared purpose.

Lore

  • T'au society is divided into five castes: Fire (warriors), Earth (builders), Water (diplomats), Air (pilots) and the mysterious Ethereals who lead them all.
  • The T'au have almost no presence in the Warp, making them highly resistant to psychic and daemonic influence.
  • Their empire grew in only a few thousand years, a heartbeat compared to other races.
  • Commander Farsight broke away from the Empire after discovering secrets about the Ethereals, forming the Farsight Enclaves.

Playstyle & Goals

Ranged, tactical and highly coordinated. T'au rely on superior firepower, markerlights, drones and battlesuits, avoiding melee whenever possible. In a roleplay setting they suit diplomats, idealists and disciplined soldiers who believe in a cause greater than themselves.

Goals

  • Spread the Greater Good across the galaxy
  • Bring other races into the empire through diplomacy or force
  • Advance their technology and expand through Spheres of Expansion
  • Protect T'au worlds from the Imperium, Tyranids and Chaos

Types of Troops

  • Fire Warriors: Core infantry armed with long-range pulse rifles and carbines.
  • Pathfinders: Scouts who mark targets with markerlights for the rest of the army.
  • Stealth Suits: Cloaked battlesuits used for infiltration and harassment.
  • Crisis Battlesuits: Versatile jet-pack battlesuits piloted by elite Fire caste warriors.
  • Broadsides: Heavy battlesuits carrying anti-armor railguns and missile systems.
  • Riptide and Stormsurge: Massive battlesuits and walking gun platforms.
  • Drones: Artificially intelligent support units providing shields, guns and sensors.
  • Ethereals: Spiritual leaders whose presence inspires all T'au.
  • Kroot: Avian mercenary auxiliaries who excel at close combat and tracking.
  • Vespid Stingwings: Insectoid auxiliaries who fly into battle with neutron blasters.
