Chaos

Khorne

About Khorne

The Blood God and Lord of Skulls, Khorne is the Chaos God of rage, war, bloodshed and martial honor. He cares not from where the blood flows, only that it flows.

Lore

  • Khorne sits upon a Brass Throne atop a mountain of skulls in his realm within the Warp.
  • He despises sorcery and psykers, seeing magic as the coward's weapon.
  • The World Eaters Legion, led by the Daemon Primarch Angron, is Khorne's most devoted Traitor Legion.
  • Khorne is the eternal enemy of Slaanesh, as rage and excess are opposed.
  • His followers shout the cry 'Blood for the Blood God! Skulls for the Skull Throne!'

Playstyle & Goals

Pure close-combat aggression. Khornate forces charge headlong into melee, gaining strength as they kill and refusing to use psychic powers. In a roleplay setting they suit berserkers, warrior-champions and characters driven by fury or a hunger for honorable combat.

Goals

  • Spill as much blood as possible in Khorne's name
  • Collect skulls for the Skull Throne
  • Prove themselves through combat against worthy foes
  • Earn daemonhood through endless slaughter

Types of Troops

  • Khorne Berzerkers: Frenzied Chaos Space Marines who live only to kill in melee.
  • World Eaters: The Traitor Legion devoted to Khorne.
  • Bloodletters: Lesser daemons of Khorne armed with hellblades.
  • Flesh Hounds: Daemonic hunting beasts immune to sorcery.
  • Bloodcrushers: Bloodletters riding brass-and-iron Juggernauts.
  • Bloodthirsters: Greater Daemons of Khorne, towering winged killers.
  • Skulltakers: Champions who gather skulls of mighty foes.
  • Cultists of Khorne: Mortal followers who fight in bloody mobs.
