Orks

About Orks

A brutal, green-skinned warrior race that lives for battle. Orks are the most widespread xenos species in the galaxy, and when gathered in vast numbers under a mighty warboss they form a WAAAGH! capable of overrunning entire sectors.

Lore

  • Orks were engineered as soldiers by the ancient Old Ones for their war against the Necrons.
  • Orks reproduce by spores, meaning that wherever they fight, more Orks eventually grow.
  • Their collective psychic field makes their beliefs real: a vehicle painted red really does go faster.
  • Orks grow larger and stronger the more they fight, and leadership goes to the biggest and toughest.

Playstyle & Goals

Loud, aggressive and chaotic. Orks overwhelm enemies with numbers and close-combat fury, backed by ramshackle but surprisingly effective technology. In a roleplay setting they reward bold, reckless and humorous characters who solve problems by charging at them.

Goals

  • Find the biggest, best fight possible
  • Loot shiny gear and better weapons (and teef, the Ork currency)
  • Grow bigger and climb the hierarchy to become a Warboss
  • Launch a WAAAGH! across the stars

Types of Troops

  • Boyz: The core mob of Ork infantry, fighting with choppas and sluggas.
  • Gretchin (Grots): Small, cowardly servants used as fodder and labor.
  • Nobz: Bigger, tougher Orks who boss the Boyz around.
  • Warboss: The biggest and meanest Ork, leader of the warband.
  • Meks: Instinctive engineers who build guns, vehicles and gadgets.
  • Painboyz: Crude surgeons who perform 'enhancements' on other Orks.
  • Weirdboyz: Ork psykers who channel the raw energy of the WAAAGH!
  • Stormboyz: Jump-pack troops who launch themselves into battle.
  • Speed Freeks: Bikers and buggy drivers obsessed with going fast.
  • Deff Dreads and Killa Kans: Walking war machines piloted by Orks or Grots.
