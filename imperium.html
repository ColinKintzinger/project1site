Imperium of Mankind

Imperium of Man

About Imperium of Man

The Imperium is ruled by the High Lords of Terra and countless institutions, from the Astra Militarum to the Inquisition. Billions of citizens toil and fight to protect humanity from the dangers of the galaxy.

Lore

  • The Emperor has been sustained on the Golden Throne for over ten thousand years after being mortally wounded by Horus.
  • The Astronomican, a psychic beacon powered by the Emperor, allows ships to navigate the Warp.
  • The Adeptus Mechanicus of Mars worship the Machine God and maintain the Imperium's technology.
  • The Inquisition hunts heretics, xenos and daemons, wielding near-absolute authority.
  • The Great Rift has split the galaxy, cutting the Imperium in half and leaving many worlds in darkness.
  • Roboute Guilliman has returned as Lord Commander of the Imperium and launched the Indomitus Crusade.

Playstyle & Goals

Massive numbers, heavy armor and diverse specialists. The Imperium's forces range from simple soldiers to towering Titans, relying on discipline, faith and firepower. In a roleplay setting this is the most flexible faction, suiting soldiers, Inquisitors, priests, tech-priests, rogue traders and ordinary citizens.

Goals

  • Protect humanity from xenos, heretics and Chaos
  • Serve and worship the God-Emperor
  • Maintain order and control across the Imperium
  • Reclaim lost worlds and technology

Types of Troops

  • Astra Militarum (Imperial Guard): Billions of human soldiers armed with lasguns.
  • Commissars: Officers who enforce discipline and loyalty.
  • Tanks and Artillery: Leman Russ battle tanks, Basilisks and Baneblades.
  • Adepta Sororitas (Sisters of Battle): Warrior nuns of the Ecclesiarchy armed with bolters and flamers.
  • Adeptus Mechanicus: Tech-priests, Skitarii and robotic war machines from Mars.
  • Imperial Knights: Towering walkers piloted by noble houses.
  • Titan Legions: Colossal god-machines that dominate the battlefield.
  • Adeptus Custodes: The Emperor's golden guardians, even mightier than Space Marines.
  • Inquisitors: Agents who hunt threats to humanity.
  • Grey Knights: Psychic Space Marines who fight daemons.
  • Officio Assassinorum: Elite assassins sent to eliminate key targets.
  • Rogue Traders: Explorers who travel beyond the Imperium's borders.
