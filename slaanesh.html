Slaanesh

About Slaanesh

The Dark Prince of Excess, Slaanesh is the Chaos God of pleasure, perfection, pride and indulgence. Born from the decadence of the ancient Aeldari, Slaanesh drives followers to push every sensation to its extreme.

Lore

  • Slaanesh was born from the Fall of the Aeldari, an event that shattered their ancient empire.
  • Slaanesh hungers for Aeldari souls above all others.
  • The Emperor's Children Legion, led by the Daemon Primarch Fulgrim, serves Slaanesh.
  • Followers chase perfection and sensation, often ending in madness.
  • Slaanesh is the youngest of the four great Chaos Gods.

Playstyle & Goals

Fast, precise and deadly. Slaaneshi forces rely on speed, agility and perfectionist skill, often striking first. In a roleplay setting they suit proud duelists, artists, perfectionists and characters driven by obsession or ego.

Goals

  • Seek perfection in every skill and pursuit
  • Indulge in ever-greater sensation and experience
  • Claim the souls of the Aeldari
  • Rise to daemonhood through excellence

Types of Troops

  • Emperor's Children: The Traitor Legion devoted to Slaanesh.
  • Noise Marines: Chaos Space Marines with sonic weapons.
  • Daemonettes: Lesser daemons of Slaanesh, swift and deadly.
  • Seekers: Daemonettes riding fast Steeds of Slaanesh.
  • Fiends: Scorpion-like daemons that hypnotize their prey.
  • Keepers of Secrets: Greater Daemons of Slaanesh, graceful and terrifying.
  • Lord Kakophonist: Champion who leads Noise Marines with sound.
  • Cultists of Slaanesh: Mortal followers chasing excess.
